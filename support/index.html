Support

Last updated November 23, 2026. Operator: Tyler Brown.

Slip Jar is a local-first betting journal for iPhone. There is no account to create or log into.

Need help or found a bug? Email x30p7j11+slipjar@mail.grokbot.com. Include your iOS version and the Slip Jar version from Settings, and please don't send personal or financial details we don't need. You can also open a public request on GitHub.

Purchases

To restore a purchase, open Slip Jar Pro in the app and tap Restore purchases. To change or cancel a subscription: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Everything is stored on your iPhone. Export a backup in Settings → Your data, and restore it from the same screen. Deleting the app deletes your data unless you have a backup.

Slip photos

Slip-photo entry reads the text on your bet slip on the device and pre-fills a bet you can check and edit. The photo is never uploaded. If something is read wrong, just fix it before saving, or log manually.

Tax log

The tax log is a record-keeping summary, not tax advice.
