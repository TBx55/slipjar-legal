Privacy Policy

Last updated November 23, 2026. Operator: Tyler Brown.

This policy describes how the Slip Jar iOS app handles information. It is not legal advice.

In short

Slip Jar is local-first. There is no account, and the app includes no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking. We do not collect data from the app: for the App Store privacy label the answer is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter — bets, odds, stakes, sportsbooks, notes, weekly reviews, settings, XP and badges — is stored only on your iPhone. We do not run a server for the app and never receive your data. Uninstalling the app deletes it unless you exported a backup. Backups and CSV exports are files you create and share yourself through the iOS share sheet; we do not host them.

Bet slip photos

If you use slip-photo entry, the app reads the photo you choose or take using Apple's on-device text recognition to pre-fill a bet you can edit. The photo is processed on your iPhone, is never uploaded, and is not saved by Slip Jar. The app asks for camera or photo library access only for this, and only when you use it.

Purchases

Slip Jar Pro and the Tax Season Pass are sold by Apple. Apple processes payment and your Apple ID; we never receive your card details. The app checks your purchase status on the device through StoreKit. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

No tracking or selling

We do not use the advertising identifier, do not track you across apps or websites, and do not sell or share personal information.

Age

Slip Jar is for adults 18+ (21+ where your local law requires) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

If you contact us

Questions about privacy: email x30p7j11+slipjar@mail.grokbot.com. If you email us, we receive only what you send (your email address and message) and use it only to reply; we don't add you to any list. If you instead open a request on GitHub, it is public and handled by GitHub under its own privacy policy. Please don't send personal or financial details you don't need to.

Changes

If this policy changes we will post the update here and change the date above.
